Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a technique that improves large language models
by allowing them to retrieve relevant documents from an external knowledge base.

RAG reduces hallucinations by grounding answers in retrieved context.
It consists of two main stages: retrieval and generation.

Vector databases store embeddings that represent semantic meaning of text.
Cosine similarity is commonly used to compare embeddings.
